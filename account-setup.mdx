After creating your Getpeeko account, a few minutes of configuration will make your experience more personal, more secure, and better suited to how you work. This page covers your profile, workspace settings, security, and notifications.

## Your profile

Your profile controls how you appear to teammates inside the workspace. To access profile settings, click your avatar in the top-right corner of any page and select **Profile settings**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Update your display name">
    In the **Display name** field, enter the name you want teammates to see. This appears in comments, activity logs, shared reports, and team invitation emails.

    Click **Save changes** when you're done.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Upload a profile photo">
    Click **Change photo** under the avatar placeholder. Select an image from your device — JPEG or PNG files up to 5 MB are supported. Getpeeko crops and resizes the image automatically.

    If you don't upload a photo, Getpeeko generates an avatar using your display name initials.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set notification preferences">
    The **Notifications** tab inside profile settings lets you choose how and when Getpeeko contacts you. You can configure email digests, real-time alerts, and project-specific notifications. See the [Notification preferences](#notification-preferences) section below for full details.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Workspace settings

Workspace settings apply to all members of your workspace. Only workspace **Admins** can change these settings. To access them, go to **Settings > Workspace**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Rename your workspace">
    Under **General**, click the **Workspace name** field and enter a new name. The name appears in the sidebar, in invitation emails, and in any exported reports.

    Click **Save** to apply the change. The updated name is visible to all members immediately.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Upload a workspace logo">
    Click **Upload logo** in the **Branding** section. Getpeeko accepts PNG or SVG files up to 2 MB. The logo appears in the sidebar and on exported reports sent to stakeholders.

    To remove the current logo and revert to the default Getpeeko branding, click **Remove logo**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure workspace-level defaults">
    Under **Defaults**, you can set the default role assigned to new members invited to the workspace, the default date range applied to new project analyses, and the default export format for reports (PDF or CSV).

    These defaults save time when onboarding new teammates or creating projects at scale. Individual members and project owners can always override them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Security

Keeping your account secure protects your data and your team's work. Getpeeko offers password management, two-factor authentication, and session controls. Find these options under **Settings > Security**.

<Tip>
  Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) on your account. Even if your password is compromised, 2FA prevents unauthorized access. Workspace Admins can also require 2FA for all members under **Settings > Workspace > Security policy**.
</Tip>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Change your password">
    Under **Password**, click **Change password**. Enter your current password, then enter and confirm your new password.

    Getpeeko requires passwords to be at least 12 characters. Using a password manager to generate a unique password is strongly recommended.

    Click **Update password** to save. You'll receive a confirmation email after the change is applied.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enable two-factor authentication">
    Under **Two-factor authentication**, click **Set up 2FA**.

    Getpeeko supports authenticator apps such as Google Authenticator, Authy, and 1Password. Scan the QR code with your authenticator app, then enter the six-digit code it generates to confirm the setup.

    Getpeeko also displays a set of one-time recovery codes — store these in a safe place. If you lose access to your authenticator app, a recovery code is the only way to regain access to your account.

    Click **Enable 2FA** to finish setup.
  </Step>

  <Step title="View active sessions">
    Under **Active sessions**, you can see every device and browser currently signed in to your account. Each entry shows the device type, browser, approximate location, and the time of last activity.

    Review this list regularly. If you see a session you don't recognize, revoke it immediately.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Revoke access">
    To sign out a specific session, click **Revoke** next to it. To sign out all other sessions at once, click **Revoke all other sessions**.

    Revoking a session signs that device out immediately. The user on that device will need to sign in again before they can access Getpeeko.

    If you believe your account has been compromised, revoke all sessions and change your password immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Notification preferences

Getpeeko can notify you about analysis results, teammate activity, and workspace events. You can configure notifications under **Settings > Notifications** or from the **Notifications** tab in your profile settings.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Configure email digests">
    Under **Email digests**, choose how often Getpeeko sends you a summary of workspace activity:

    - **Daily** — a digest sent each morning with new insights, completed analyses, and team activity from the past 24 hours.
    - **Weekly** — a summary sent every Monday covering the previous week.
    - **Off** — no digest emails.

    Select your preferred cadence and click **Save**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enable real-time alerts">
    Real-time alerts notify you by email or in-app as events happen. Toggle the alerts you want to receive:

    - **Analysis complete** — notified when an analysis you triggered finishes running.
    - **New insight flagged** — notified when a high-confidence insight is detected in any project you have access to.
    - **Teammate comment** — notified when a teammate comments on an insight or report you're involved in.
    - **Integration error** — notified when a connected data source fails to sync.

    In-app notifications appear in the bell icon at the top of the dashboard. Email notifications go to the address associated with your account.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set project-specific notifications">
    For more granular control, open a project and go to **Project settings > Notifications**. From there you can configure notification rules that apply only to that project — for example, receiving immediate alerts for insights in a critical project while only getting weekly digests for others.

    Project-specific settings take precedence over your global notification preferences.
  </Step>
</Steps>
