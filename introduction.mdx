## How it works

Getpeeko organizes your work into **projects** inside a shared **workspace**. Each project connects to your data sources, runs AI-powered analysis, and surfaces actionable insights about your audience.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at [app.getpeeko.ai](https://app.getpeeko.ai) and set up your workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a project">
    Create a project and define your target audience or data source.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run an analysis">
    Let Getpeeko's AI engine surface behavioral patterns and insights.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share and act">
    Export results, share reports with your team, and set up alerts.
  </Step>
</Steps>
