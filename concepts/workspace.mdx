A workspace is the shared environment your team works in. It contains all your projects, members, and data source integrations, and it has its own billing, settings, and access controls. When you first sign up for Getpeeko, you create a workspace as part of onboarding. Everyone you invite to collaborate joins the workspace and inherits one of the available roles.

## Creating a workspace

You can create additional workspaces at any time from your account settings. Each workspace operates independently with its own projects, members, and billing.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open workspace settings">
    Click your workspace name in the top-left corner of the dashboard, then select **Create new workspace** from the dropdown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your workspace">
    Enter a name for the workspace. Choose something that clearly identifies the team, client, or business unit it belongs to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a plan">
    Select the plan that matches your team's needs. You can change your plan later from **Settings → Billing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite members">
    Add team members by email. Assign each person a role — Owner, Admin, or Member — based on their responsibilities. You can invite more members after setup.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your first integration">
    Link at least one data source so your projects have data to analyze. You can add more integrations at any time from **Settings → Integrations**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Workspace members and roles

Every workspace member has one of three roles. Roles control what a member can see and do within the workspace.

| Role | Permissions |
|------|-------------|
| **Owner** | Full access to all workspace features. Can manage billing, delete the workspace, and transfer ownership to another member. There is exactly one Owner per workspace. |
| **Admin** | Can invite and remove members, create and delete projects, and manage integrations. Cannot access billing or delete the workspace. |
| **Member** | Can view projects, trigger analysis runs, view insights, and export reports. Cannot manage members, integrations, or workspace settings. |

To change a member's role, go to **Settings → Team**, find the member, and select a new role from the dropdown. Only Owners and Admins can change roles.

## Switching workspaces

If you belong to more than one workspace, you can switch between them without signing out.

Click your workspace name in the top-left corner of the dashboard. A dropdown lists all workspaces you have access to. Select the workspace you want to switch to, and the dashboard reloads with that workspace's projects and data.

The URL updates to reflect the active workspace, so you can bookmark specific workspaces for quick access.

## Workspace limits

The number of projects, members, and analysis runs available to your workspace depends on your plan.

<Note>
  Check your current usage at any time by going to **Settings → Billing**. If you approach a limit, Getpeeko displays a warning in the dashboard. Upgrade your plan to increase limits without losing any existing data or settings.
</Note>

Typical limits across plans include:

- **Projects** — the number of active projects you can maintain in the workspace
- **Members** — the number of users you can invite to the workspace
- **Analysis runs per month** — the total number of runs that can execute across all projects in a billing period

<Tip>
  If you manage multiple clients or separate business units, create a dedicated workspace for each. This keeps data, billing, and access controls cleanly separated, and lets you invite client-specific team members without exposing unrelated projects.
</Tip>
