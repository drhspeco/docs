Billing in Getpeeko is managed at the workspace level. Only the workspace **Owner** can access billing settings — Admins and Members do not see the **Billing** section in Settings. If you need to make a billing change and you are not the Owner, ask your workspace Owner or have them [transfer ownership](/settings/team#transferring-ownership) to you.

## Viewing your current plan

To see your workspace's plan and usage, navigate to **Settings** > **Billing**. The overview shows:

- **Current plan** — The name of your active plan (for example, Free, Starter, or Growth).
- **Next renewal date** — The date your subscription renews and your card is charged.
- **Usage this billing period** — A breakdown of your current consumption, including the number of projects created, total workspace members, and analysis runs used this month. Usage resets at the start of each billing period.

## Upgrading or changing your plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the plan selector">
    Navigate to **Settings** > **Billing**, then click **Change Plan**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare available plans">
    Review the plan comparison table. Each plan lists its member limit, monthly analysis runs, and included features.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a plan">
    Click **Select** under the plan you want to switch to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm payment">
    Review the billing summary and click **Confirm**. Upgrades are applied immediately and prorated — you are charged only for the remaining days in your current billing period. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period; you keep your existing plan's features until then.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If you need a custom plan, higher usage limits, or enterprise-level features such as SSO or dedicated support, contact the Getpeeko team at [support@getpeeko.ai](mailto:support@getpeeko.ai). Enterprise pricing is available on request.
</Note>

## Payment methods

To add or update your payment details, navigate to **Settings** > **Billing** > **Payment Method** and click **Update Payment Method**.

Getpeeko uses [Stripe](https://stripe.com) to process all payments securely. Your card details are stored and managed by Stripe — Getpeeko never stores your full card number. The following card types are accepted:

- Visa
- Mastercard
- American Express

You can save one payment method per workspace. To replace an existing card, enter your new card details and click **Save**. The new card is used for the next billing cycle.

## Invoices and receipts

Getpeeko generates an invoice automatically at the start of each billing period. Invoices are emailed to the workspace Owner's email address on the renewal date.

To download a past invoice manually:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Invoices">
    Navigate to **Settings** > **Billing** > **Invoices**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the invoice">
    Locate the billing period you need in the invoice list. Each entry shows the date, amount, and status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download the PDF">
    Click **Download** next to the invoice to save a PDF copy suitable for accounting or expense purposes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Cancelling your subscription

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing settings">
    Navigate to **Settings** > **Billing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Cancel Subscription">
    Scroll to the bottom of the page and click **Cancel Subscription**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a cancellation reason">
    Optionally select a reason from the dropdown — this helps the Getpeeko team improve the product.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm cancellation">
    Click **Confirm Cancellation** to finalize.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period. You retain access to your plan's features until that date. After cancellation, your workspace is downgraded to the Free tier. All workspace data — projects, insights, and reports — is retained for **90 days** following cancellation. After 90 days, your data is permanently and irreversibly deleted. Export anything you need before this window closes.
</Warning>

<Note>
  For billing questions, disputes, or refund requests, contact [support@getpeeko.ai](mailto:support@getpeeko.ai). Include your workspace name and the email address associated with your account to help the team resolve your request quickly.
</Note>
