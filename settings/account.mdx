Your account settings control everything personal to your Getpeeko login — your profile details, how you authenticate, and which devices currently have access. These settings apply to your individual account and are separate from workspace-level settings.

## Profile

Your profile information is visible to other members of any workspace you belong to. To update it, navigate to **Settings** > **Account** > **Profile**.

- **Display name** — Enter the name you want teammates to see next to your activity, comments, and reports.
- **Email address** — Your email is used to log in and receive notifications. Changing your email address triggers a verification email sent to the new address. Your email does not update until you confirm the link in that message.
- **Profile photo** — Upload a JPEG or PNG image. Photos are cropped to a square and displayed at small sizes throughout the app, so use a clear, centered image.

## Password & security

### Change your password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Security settings">
    Navigate to **Settings** > **Account** > **Security**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the password form">
    Click **Change Password**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your current password">
    Type your existing password in the **Current password** field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a new password">
    Enter and confirm your new password.     Passwords must be at least 12 characters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the change">
    Click **Update Password**. You will remain logged in on the current device. Other active sessions are not terminated automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Enable two-factor authentication

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Security settings">
    Navigate to **Settings** > **Account** > **Security**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the 2FA setup">
    Under **Two-Factor Authentication**, click **Enable**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan the QR code">
    Open your authenticator app (such as Google Authenticator, Authy, or 1Password) and scan the QR code displayed on screen. If you cannot scan it, click **Enter code manually** to copy the setup key.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the code">
    Enter the six-digit code generated by your authenticator app and click **Verify**. Once confirmed, 2FA is active on your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your recovery codes">
    Copy and store the recovery codes shown after setup. These codes let you regain access if you lose your authenticator device.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Enable two-factor authentication on every account that has access to production workspaces. It is the single most effective step you can take to protect your Getpeeko data from unauthorized access.
</Tip>

## Active sessions

The **Active sessions** panel, found at **Settings** > **Account** > **Security** > **Sessions**, lists every device and browser currently signed in to your account. Each entry shows the device type, approximate location, and the time of last activity.

- **Revoke a single session** — Click **Revoke** next to any session you do not recognize or no longer need. That device is signed out immediately.
- **Revoke all other sessions** — Click **Sign out all other sessions** to terminate every session except the one you are currently using. Use this if you believe your account has been accessed without your permission.

## Delete account

<Warning>
  Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Once you delete your account, your personal profile, login credentials, and notification preferences are removed immediately and cannot be recovered.
</Warning>

Before deleting your account, consider whether you need to transfer ownership of any workspaces you own. If you are the sole Owner of a workspace, you must either transfer ownership to another member or delete the workspace itself before your account can be removed. Workspace data — including projects, insights, and reports — is retained under the remaining members if at least one other Owner exists.

To delete your account:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the Danger Zone">
    Navigate to **Settings** > **Account** > **Danger Zone**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Delete Account">
    Click **Delete Account** and read the confirmation prompt carefully.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm deletion">
    Type your email address to confirm, then click **Permanently Delete Account**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
