This guide walks you through everything you need to go from a blank slate to your first Getpeeko insight. By the end, you'll have a workspace configured, a project created, and your first AI analysis complete.

<Note>
  Getpeeko offers a free trial that includes full access to all features for 14 days. No credit card is required to sign up.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up for an account">
    Go to [app.getpeeko.ai](https://app.getpeeko.ai) and click **Get started**.

    You can create an account using an email address and password, or sign in with an SSO provider such as Google or Microsoft. After submitting your details, check your inbox for a verification email and click the confirmation link to activate your account.

    If you don't receive the email within a few minutes, check your spam folder or click **Resend verification email** on the sign-in page.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create your workspace">
    After verifying your email, Getpeeko prompts you to create your first workspace.

    Enter a workspace name — this is usually your company or team name, and it will appear across the dashboard and in any team invitations you send. Then select a plan. You can start on the free trial and upgrade at any time from **Settings > Billing**.

    Click **Create workspace** to continue.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Invite your team">
    You can invite teammates before you create your first project, or skip this step and do it later.

    To invite someone now, go to **Settings > Team** and click **Invite member**. Enter their email address, choose a role — **Admin** or **Member** — and click **Send invite**. They'll receive an email with a link to join your workspace.

    <Tip>
      You can invite as many teammates as your plan allows. Members can view and run projects and export reports. Admins get additional permissions to manage integrations and invite others.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create your first project">
    From the workspace home, click **New project**. Give the project a descriptive name that reflects the audience or question you're investigating — for example, "Onboarding drop-off Q3" or "Power user behavioral patterns."

    Next, select your data source or audience type. Getpeeko supports a range of integrations including product analytics platforms, CRM exports, and direct data uploads. If you haven't connected a data source yet, you can select **Sample data** to explore Getpeeko with a pre-loaded dataset.

    Click **Create project** to save and continue.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Run your first analysis">
    Inside your project, click **Run analysis**. Getpeeko's AI engine processes your data and identifies behavioral patterns, audience segments, and notable trends.

    Most analyses complete in under 60 seconds. A progress indicator shows you where the analysis is in the pipeline. You'll receive a notification in the app (and optionally by email) when results are ready.

    If the analysis fails, Getpeeko displays an error message with a suggested action — usually a data formatting issue or a missing field in your source data.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Explore your insights">
    Once the analysis finishes, open the **Insights** panel. This is where Getpeeko surfaces AI-generated findings about your audience — things like behavioral clusters, engagement patterns, drop-off points, and anomalies.

    Each insight card shows a summary, the supporting data behind the finding, and a confidence score. Click any card to expand it and see a detailed breakdown. You can pin insights to share them with your team, export them to a report, or add them to a dashboard.

    Use the filter controls at the top of the panel to narrow insights by type, date range, or segment.
  </Step>
</Steps>
